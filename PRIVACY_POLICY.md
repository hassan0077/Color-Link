### 1. Introduction
Welcome to **Color Link**. Your privacy is critically important to us. This Privacy Policy outlines how your information is handled when you download, install, and play the **Color Link** mobile application.

Our fundamental principle is simple: **We do not collect, process, store, or share any personal information about you.**

---

### 2. Data Collection and Usage
**Color Link** is designed as a standalone offline logic puzzle game.

* **Personal Identifiable Information (PII):** We **do not** collect personal details such as your name, email address, phone number, location data, IP address, or device ID.
* **Usage Data:** We **do not** track your gameplay activity, button clicks, session duration, or device telemetry.
* **Third-Party Analytics:** We **do not** integrate any third-party analytics services (such as Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, or Flurry).

---

### 3. Local Data Storage
All game data is saved **100% locally on your device** using Android's native local storage (`SharedPreferences`).

The only data stored on your device includes:
* Level completion status (unlocked levels and star ratings earned).
* Best moves and elapsed time per level.
* Your selected theme preference (Light or Dark mode).

This data never leaves your device and is automatically erased if you uninstall or clear the app's storage.

---

### 4. Device Permissions
**Color Link** operates offline and requires **zero sensitive permissions**:
* **No** internet or network access required.
* **No** camera or microphone permissions.
* **No** location permissions.
* **No** contact or file storage access permissions.

### 5. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & GDPR Compliance)
Our application is family-friendly and suitable for players of all ages (Target Age: 3+ / Everyone). 

Because **Color Link** collects zero personal information from any user, it fully complies with the **Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)** and the **General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)** regarding minors.

### 6. Third-Party Services & Advertising
* **Advertisements:** **Color Link** contains no advertisements, ad networks, or ad tracking code.
* **External Links:** The app does not contain external hyperlinks to third-party websites or services.
